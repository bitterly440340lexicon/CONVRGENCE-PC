## 🕹️ About

CONVRGENCE is a single-player VR extraction shooter developed and published by Monkey-With-a-Bomb. Set in the procedurally generated Chernokamensk Anomaly Zone, it follows an artifact hunter investigating a mysterious exclusion zone filled with bandits, wild animals, and nocturnal demons. Players prepare raids, customize realistic firearms, scavenge valuables and artifacts, then extract before death or nightfall. A loyal dog companion assists with combat and scavenging while diegetic interfaces and changing locations deepen the immersive survival experience.

CONVRGENCE is made for VR players who enjoy atmospheric exploration, realistic firearms, survival horror, and high-risk single-player extraction gameplay.

## ✨ Features

- 🎯 **Extraction Gameplay** — Plan raids, gather valuable loot, and extract safely before danger overwhelms you.
- 🗺️ **Anomaly Zone** — Explore procedurally generated locations that make every expedition unpredictable.
- 🔧 **Weapon Customization** — Maintain and modify a realistic arsenal to suit different threats and tactics.
- 🧭 **Immersive VR Design** — Use diegetic interfaces, physical interactions, and flexible movement options.
- 🐕 **Dog Companion** — Rely on a loyal companion to help in combat and locate useful supplies.
- 🌙 **Nighttime Threats** — Face mysterious demons and escalating danger when darkness falls over the Zone.
